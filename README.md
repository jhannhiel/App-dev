# Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba

# Overview
Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba is a popular anime series that follows the story of Tanjiro Kamado, a kind-hearted boy who becomes a demon slayer after his family is slaughtered by demons and his sister, Nezuko, is turned into one.

# Why i like it:
- The characters are unique and memorable
- Great Fight scene
- Amazing Animation

# Main Characters
- Kamado Tanjiro – A determined and kind demon slayer.
- Kamado Nezuko – Tanjiro’s sister who became a demon.
- Agatsuma Zenitsu – A scared but powerful fighter
- Hashibira Inosuke - A wild and strong swordsman with boar head mask
